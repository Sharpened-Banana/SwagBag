# SwagBag 0.17: in-game test checklists

Six sessions: AG1, AG2, AG3 (alt gold and currencies), IC1 (item-level colors), PB1, PB2 (the per-bag view). Do AG1 and AG2 first, on two characters; AG3 only if you run Syndicator.

## Before you start

- 0.17 needs the 0.17 build. Claude installs the build first: say "install 0.17".
- After that, fully quit the game and start it again (new files; a `/reload` is not enough). The login line must say v0.17.0.
- If anything shows a red error, type `/swagbag report`, press Ctrl+C, and paste the output to Claude.
- After each session, reply "<session> done" plus the numbers of any steps that didn't match. For example: `AG1 done, steps 3 and 6 didn't match`.

Chat lines in these checklists start with `SwagBag: `.

You need: two characters (call them A and B) with some gold on each. Session AG2 needs at least one crest or other currency on both. Session PB2 needs a banker and, for the Warband part, the Warband bank unlocked.

---

## Session AG1: gold of all characters (no Syndicator)

Purpose: the gold tooltip lists every character that has logged in with 0.17. Turn Syndicator off for this session (disable it on the AddOns screen) so SwagBag uses its own saved data.

1. Log in on character A. Expected: chat shows `SwagBag: v0.17.0 loaded.`
2. Open your bags and hover the gold in the footer. Expected: a tooltip with `Gold on this character` and, under it, a line saying `Log in on your other characters once to add them here.`
3. Log out and log in on character B. Open the bags.
4. Hover the gold. Expected: the tooltip lists character A and character B, richest first, each with its gold in the class color.
5. Look at the bottom of the tooltip. Expected: if you have ever opened the Warband bank with 0.17, a `Warband bank` line; then a `Total` line.
6. Check the Total by hand: A's gold + B's gold (+ the Warband bank line). Expected: it matches.
7. Without opening the bank, log out and in again, and hover the gold. Expected: the Warband bank line is still there, with the amount you last saw.
8. Look at the footer itself. Expected: it looks the same as before 0.17.

If a character is missing, or the Total is wrong: tell Claude which character and what you saw.

---

## Session AG2: currencies and crests

Purpose: the currency and crest tooltips list every character too.

1. On character B, hover a currency in the footer. Expected: the tooltip shows the currency, then each character that has some, most first, with the amount. The first line is your own.
2. Hover a crest in the footer (if you have crests). Expected: the same kind of list.
3. Log in on character A, hover the same currency. Expected: B is listed with the amount B had when you left.
4. Spend or gain a little of that currency on A, wait a few seconds, switch to B and hover again. Expected: A's new amount.
5. A character that has none of that currency is not listed.

If a character shows the wrong amount, or a currency you own is missing: tell Claude which one.

---

## Session AG3: with Syndicator

Purpose: with Syndicator on, every alt is listed at once.

1. Turn Syndicator on and fully restart the game. Log in on a character that has not logged in with 0.17.
2. Hover the gold in the footer. Expected: every character Syndicator knows is listed at once, not only A and B.
3. Compare three characters' gold with Syndicator's own display (its tooltip or Baganator's). Expected: the same amounts.
4. Hover a crest or currency. Expected: all characters that own it are listed.
5. Turn Syndicator off again and restart. Expected: the tooltip falls back to the characters SwagBag has seen.

If the amounts differ from Syndicator's: tell Claude the character and both numbers.

## Session IC1: item-level colors

Purpose: item levels take a color by step, filled from your equipped item level. It is off until you turn it on.

1. Before you turn it on, look at item levels on your gear icons. Expected: white, as always.
2. Type `/swagbag ilvlcolors on`. Expected: chat shows `SwagBag: Item levels are colored by your steps.`
3. Look at the gear in your bags. Expected: item levels are colored: gray or white below your equipped level, green from your equipped level, blue from 10 above it, purple from 20 above it.
4. Open the List view. Expected: the item level column is colored the same way.
5. Open Settings (`/swagbag settings`) > Overlays. Expected: a `Color item levels` box (ticked) and up to four rows, each with a level and a color.
6. Change one level number (for example, make the blue step start higher) and pick another color for a step. Expected: the bags change at once; chat shows `Item level colors updated.`
7. Type a bad level such as `abc` in a row. Expected: a message asking for an item level from 1 to 9999, and nothing else changes.
8. Type `/swagbag ilvlcolors off`. Expected: chat shows `Item levels are white again. Your steps are kept.` and all item levels are white.
9. Type `/swagbag ilvlcolors on` again. Expected: your changed steps come back (not re-filled).

If the colors are wrong or the numbers don't match your equipped level: tell Claude your equipped item level and what you see.

## Session PB1: the bags window, one section per bag

Purpose: the Bags view, and the two safety checks that make sure nothing moves under your mouse.

1. Open your bags and click the view button until it reads `Bags` (it cycles Categories, List, Grid, Bags). Or type `/swagbag view bags`.
2. Look at the headers. Expected: one section per equipped bag: the Backpack first, then Bag 1 and so on (or each bag's own name), the reagent bag last. Each header reads like `Backpack · 16 slots · 3 free`. A bag slot with no bag has no section.
3. Check one bag by hand: the slots and free numbers match what Blizzard's bag shows.
4. Click a bag's header. Expected: it folds to one line. Click again and it opens.
5. Fold one bag, type `/reload`, open the bags. Expected: it is still folded.
6. Type part of an item's name in the search box. Expected: the other items dim and nothing moves.
7. Safety check, fold under the mouse: leave your mouse resting on a bag's header and click it. Expected: it folds at once, and nothing jumps under the mouse.
8. Safety check, switch under the mouse: type `/swagbag view sections` to go back to Categories. Rest your mouse on an item in your bags and do not move it. Press Enter, type `/swagbag view bags`, and press Enter again. Expected: the view does not switch while the mouse is on the item and nothing moves under it. Move the mouse off the item: the Bags view appears.
9. Pick up an item and drop it on another slot in the Bags view. Expected: it moves normally.
10. Type `/swagbag view sections`. Expected: the category view is back.

If a header's numbers are wrong, a fold is not remembered, or anything moves under the mouse: tell Claude the step.

## Session PB2: the bank, one section per tab

Purpose: the bank's Bags view, with each tab named as in Blizzard's bank.

1. Open the bank. Click `...` and pick `Bags`. Expected: the Character bank shows one section per bank tab, each named as you named it in Blizzard's bank (an unnamed tab shows `Tab 1`, `Tab 2`...). Each header shows slots and free slots.
2. A tab you have not bought has no section.
3. Click `Warband Bank`. Expected: the Warband bank shows one section per tab too, with its names.
4. Click a tab's header. Expected: it folds. Close the bank and open it again: it is still folded.
5. Safety check, fold under the mouse: rest your mouse on a tab's header and click. Expected: it folds at once, and nothing jumps under the mouse.
6. Switch views from the menu: click `...` and pick `Categories`, then `...` and `Bags` again. Expected: each switch redraws the bank at once (the menu is not over an item), with your folded tabs still folded.
7. Right-click an item in your bags. Expected: it still goes to the bank that is lit, as before.
8. Pick `Categories` from `...`. Expected: the category view is back.

If a tab name is wrong, a bought tab is missing, or right-click deposit stops working: tell Claude the step.
